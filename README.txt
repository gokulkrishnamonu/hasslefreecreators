Before going live:
1. Replace hello@hasslefreecreators.com and social links with real accounts.
2. Add a real assets/og-cover.jpg (1200x630px) for social share previews.
3. Update https://www.hasslefreecreators.com in canonical/og/schema tags to your live domain.
4. Connect the contact form (contact.html) to a real backend or form service
   (e.g. Formspree, Netlify Forms) — it currently shows a success message only.
5. Update sitemap.xml dates/URLs to match your final domain and submit it in
   Google Search Console.

To preview: open index.html in a browser, or serve the folder locally
(e.g. `python3 -m http.server`) so relative asset paths resolve correctly.
